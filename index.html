About Forge

BUILT DIFFERENT.
FOR A REASON.

LinkedIn is a resume hall. Twitter is noise. There's no platform built specifically for the startup ecosystem — until now.

Forge is a professional networking app designed for founders, investors, and aspiring entrepreneurs. A focused space where the right conversations happen between the right people.

Not social media. Not a job board. A network with purpose.

01
Real Conversations
Founders posting about real milestones. Investors sharing where their money is going. Aspiring entrepreneurs learning in public from people who've done it.
02
Honest Community
A confessions and advice layer where founders share failures, lessons, and what they wish they knew. Raw. Honest. Useful.
03
Built to Connect
Smart search by niche, region, and role. Professional messaging with calls and meetings. Company pages. All in one place.
What We're Building

SIX PILLARS
OF FORGE

01
Startup Feed
A focused feed for founders posting updates, investors announcing deals, and builders sharing lessons. Role-tagged, niche-filtered, signal-only.
02
Confessions & Advice
The most honest section of any professional platform. Founders post real mistakes. Investors share what they wish they'd known. No pretending.
03
Smart Discovery
Search people by role, region, language, age, and niche. Find exactly who you need — the investor in your space, the co-founder in your city.
04
Pro Messaging
Direct messages, voice calls, voicemail, and meeting scheduling. A full professional inbox designed for high-stakes conversations between serious people.
05
Company Pages
Verified pages for startups. Post updates, attract talent, build credibility. Accessible to founders with an active website and LinkedIn.
06
Leaderboard & Rewards
Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly boards. Community votes on the most helpful advice. Real recognition for people who add real value.
